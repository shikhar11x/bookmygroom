# BookMyGroom Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

BookMyGroom ("we", "the app") is operated by Shikhar bajpai, L=Delhi , India. Contact: shikhar11x@gmail.com.

## What we collect
- **Account:** name and email address.
- **Bookings:** services, stylist, date and time, studio, price and payment method.
- **Payments:** online payments are processed by Razorpay. We do not store card, UPI or bank details. We store the payment status and reference IDs.
- **Reviews:** ratings, optional comments and optional photos you upload.
- **Loyalty:** points earned from completed visits.
- **Device token:** to send push notifications about your bookings.

## How we use it
To create and manage your bookings, process payments, check you in at the studio, send booking notifications and reminders, show your loyalty points, and show verified ratings for stylists.

## Who we share it with
Service providers who run the app for us: Supabase (database and login), Razorpay (payments) and Google Firebase (push notifications). We do not sell your data.

## Camera and photos
The camera is used only by studio staff to scan check-in codes. Photos are used only if you choose to attach one to a review.

## Retention and deletion
You can delete your account in the app (Profile, then Delete account). This permanently removes your account, bookings, reviews, points and device tokens. Payment records may be retained by Razorpay as required by law.

You can ask us to access or correct your data by emailing [your email].

## Children
The app is not intended for children under 13.
